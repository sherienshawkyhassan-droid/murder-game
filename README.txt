MURDER AT BLACKWOOD MANOR
=========================

You are the lead detective in a locked-room murder investigation.
Examine the crime scene, collect evidence, question three suspects,
survive a timed confrontation, and accuse the murderer.

PLAY
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Open the Blackwood_Manor_Game folder.
3. Double-click install_and_play.bat.

CREATE AN EXE
1. Double-click build_exe.bat.
2. Wait for SUCCESS.
3. Open the dist folder.
4. Double-click Murder_at_the_Blackwood_Manor.exe.
